Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2025

At Java Master, we believe in keeping things simple. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the minimal information we collect. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

In Short: We only collect your username and password to create your account. We don't ask for emails, addresses, or any other personal information.

What Information Do We Collect?

When you create an account on Java Master, we collect only the following:

  • Username: A username you choose to identify your account
  • Password: A password you create to secure your account (stored encrypted)
  • Progress Data: Information about your learning progress, completed tasks, and achievements within the platform
  • Login Activity: Basic information about when you log in to track your account activity

What we DON'T collect: We do not collect or require email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, payment information, or any other personal identifying information.

How Do We Use Your Information?

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account
  • To authenticate you when you log in
  • To track and save your learning progress
  • To show you your completed tasks and achievements
  • To improve the learning experience on our platform

How Do We Protect Your Information?

We take the security of your information seriously:

  • Your password is encrypted and never stored in plain text
  • We use secure methods to store and protect your data
  • Access to user data is restricted and monitored

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Do We Share Your Information?

No. We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your information with third parties. Your username and progress data stay with us and are used solely to provide you with the Java Master learning experience.

Cookies and Tracking

We may use cookies or similar technologies to:

  • Keep you logged in to your account
  • Remember your preferences
  • Understand how you use the platform to improve it

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect your ability to use certain features of Java Master.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data: View what information we have about your account
  • Delete your account: Request complete deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Update your information: Change your username or password at any time

Children's Privacy

Java Master is intended for users of all ages learning Java programming. Since we don't collect personal information like email addresses, our service is safe for younger users. However, we recommend parental guidance for users under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please contact us through the Java Master platform.

Summary: Java Master keeps it simple. We only need your username and password to get you started learning Java. No emails, no personal details—just you and your coding journey.
